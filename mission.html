Company statement

Our mission & goals

Why BlendWorks exists, what we promise, and where we are going.

Mission

Give every person the power to build the exact supplement their body and goals call for — transparently, safely, and simply.

The problem we exist to fix

Serious training ends up with a counter full of bottles — four, five, six products, each with its own label, its own reorder date and, too often, a "proprietary blend" that hides how much of anything you're really getting. One-size-fits-all formulas give a 52 kg lifter and a 100 kg lifter the same scoop. And every extra bottle makes it less likely you'll actually take any of it.

What we do about it

One blend, one bottle, one habit. BlendWorks makes capsule and powder blends where every milligram is on the label, doses match the studies, and every bottle carries a lot number you can trace. Choose from our in-stock formulas, or use the Blend Builder to set the ingredients and the ratios yourself — and we'll make it for you.

Built by you. Blended by us. Nothing hidden.
GOAL 01

Make personalisation the default

A custom formula should be as easy and as affordable as an ordinary bottle off the shelf — not a luxury.

GOAL 02

Replace guesswork with transparent dosing

Every ingredient, every milligram, on the label and on the screen — with clear guidance on studied ranges.

GOAL 03

Collapse the routine to one product

Fewer bottles means better adherence, better results, and a habit that actually sticks.

GOAL 04

Earn trust through quality

Small batches, identity-tested ingredient lots, lot numbers on every bottle, published certificates of analysis.

GOAL 05

Become the home of your formula

A formula that lives in your account and evolves with your training, your coach, and your goals.

Values

What we won't compromise on

01

Transparency

No hidden blends, no fairy-dusting, no marketing doses. If it's in the bottle, it's on the label with a number.

02

Evidence over hype

We use the ingredients and doses that human studies support, and we say plainly when the evidence is thin.

03

Safety before sales

Hard caps on stimulants, clear warnings, human quality checks on every batch. Custom never means careless.

04

Simplicity

One product, one routine. Every feature we build has to make the daily habit easier, not harder.

05

Ownership

Your formula belongs to you. Name it, share it, change it whenever your training changes.

06

Honesty about limits

If a formula needs five capsules a day, we say five. If an ingredient works better as a powder, we make it a powder.
