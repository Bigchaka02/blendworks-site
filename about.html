About BlendWorks

Supplements, without the guesswork

We started BlendWorks because the smartest lifters we know were still buying six bottles and reading six labels — and still didn't know what was in their pre-workout.

The story

BlendWorks began with a simple, slightly annoyed question: why can I customise my running shoes, my skincare and my coffee order, but not the one thing I put in my body every single morning?

The supplement aisle answers with fixed formulas, "proprietary blends" that hide the numbers, and one scoop for everyone. So we set out to build the opposite: blends where every milligram is printed on the label, where doses match the research, and where you can set the ingredients and ratios yourself.

We make everything in small batches, to order. Each ingredient lot is identity-tested before it's used, every bottle carries a lot number, and every batch gets a human quality check before it ships.

Where we are today

A line of in-stock capsule and powder blends, a shop that's live, and a Blend Builder where you design your own. We're a small team with big opinions about labels.

How we make it

Small batches, big transparency

  • Ingredients arriveEach lot is quarantined, its certificate of analysis reviewed, and its identity tested before release.
  • Weighed & blendedCalibrated balances, validated blend times, potent ingredients pre-diluted for even distribution.
  • Encapsulated or packedCapsules are filled and weight-checked; powders are blended and sealed with a desiccant.
  • Checked & labelledA second set of eyes reviews the batch record before the label — with its lot number — goes on.
  • Shipped to youSealed, packed and on its way within a few business days.
