FAQ

Common questions

Why do some capsule blends need 5 capsules a day?
Because we use full doses. Creatine, citrulline and beta-alanine work at gram-level amounts, and a capsule holds well under a gram. We'd rather say "five capsules" than fairy-dust an ingredient into one.
Can I design my own blend?
Yes — open Build your own: pick capsules or powder, choose the size, add 2–6 ingredients and set their ratios in 10% steps. Every in-stock blend also shows its full formula.
Are the ingredients tested?
Every ingredient lot is identity-tested before use and every bottle carries a lot number. Certificates of analysis will be published per lot as production starts.
Do the powders contain sugar or artificial colours?
No sugar and no artificial colours. Flavoured powders use natural flavours and a non-nutritive sweetener; exact "other ingredients" will be listed on each label.
Where do you ship?
United States to start. Final shipping regions, rates and delivery times will be confirmed before launch.
What if I don't like a blend?
Tell us. Our return and satisfaction policy is being finalised; the intent is a credit toward a different blend on your first order.
